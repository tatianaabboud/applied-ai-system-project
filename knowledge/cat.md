# Cat care guidelines

## Kitten feeding
species: cat | age: kitten | topic: feeding

Kittens under 6 months need 3-4 small meals a day. From 6 to 12 months, 3 meals a day is fine, moving to 2 as they near one year. Each feeding takes about 5-10 minutes.

Source: cat.md, Kitten feeding

## Adult cat feeding
species: cat | age: adult | topic: feeding

Adult cats do well on 2 meals a day, about 12 hours apart (for example 07:30 and 19:30). Each feeding takes about 5-10 minutes. Clean the water bowl daily and the litter box at least once a day (about 5 minutes).

Source: cat.md, Adult cat feeding

## Senior cat feeding
species: cat | age: senior | topic: feeding

Senior cats often do better with 3-4 small meals a day. Watch for weight loss and for changes in drinking, and report them to a vet.

Source: cat.md, Senior cat feeding

## Kitten play
species: cat | age: kitten | topic: exercise

Kittens need 3-4 short play sessions of 10-15 minutes a day, using wand toys and chase games. Short sessions suit them better than one long one.

Source: cat.md, Kitten play

## Adult cat play
species: cat | age: adult | topic: exercise

Adult cats need 2 play sessions of 10-15 minutes a day (about 20-30 minutes total), ideally before meals to mimic hunting. Indoor-only cats need more play than cats with outdoor access.

Source: cat.md, Adult cat play

## Senior cat play
species: cat | age: senior | topic: exercise

Senior cats enjoy gentler play: 2-3 sessions of 5-10 minutes a day with slow-moving toys. Keep sessions short and let the cat set the pace.

Source: cat.md, Senior cat play

## Cat grooming
species: cat | age: all | topic: grooming

Brush short-haired cats once or twice a week (5 minutes) and long-haired cats daily (5-10 minutes). Trim nails every 2-3 weeks (about 5 minutes). Brush teeth several times a week if the cat allows it.

Source: cat.md, Cat grooming

## Cat enrichment
species: cat | age: all | topic: enrichment

Rotate toys weekly, and offer a window perch or climbing spot. Puzzle feeders or scattered treats give 10 minutes of useful activity for indoor cats.

Source: cat.md, Cat enrichment
