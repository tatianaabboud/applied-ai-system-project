# Other pets: general care guidelines

Use these when the species is not a dog or cat (for example rabbit, guinea pig, hamster, bird or reptile). Species-specific needs vary a lot, so these are only a starting routine.

## General small-pet feeding
species: other | age: all | topic: feeding

Feed small mammals fresh food and water once or twice a day (about 5-10 minutes), at the same times each day. Rabbits and guinea pigs should always have hay available. Remove uneaten fresh food after a few hours.

Source: other_pets.md, General small-pet feeding

## Young small-pet feeding
species: other | age: young | topic: feeding

Young animals under about 6 months usually need more frequent feeding, 3 times a day, with food made for their age.

Source: other_pets.md, Young small-pet feeding

## General small-pet exercise
species: other | age: adult | topic: exercise

Rabbits and guinea pigs need supervised time outside the cage: at least 1-3 hours a day for rabbits and 30-60 minutes for guinea pigs. Hamsters need a wheel and 15-20 minutes of handling or play.

Source: other_pets.md, General small-pet exercise

## General cage care
species: other | age: all | topic: grooming

Spot-clean the cage or enclosure daily (5-10 minutes) and do a full clean weekly (20-30 minutes). Check nails, teeth and coat weekly (5 minutes).

Source: other_pets.md, General cage care

## Small-pet enrichment
species: other | age: all | topic: enrichment

Offer chew toys, tunnels or foraging games for 10-15 minutes a day, and change them regularly.

Source: other_pets.md, Small-pet enrichment
