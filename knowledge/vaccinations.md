# Vaccination and checkup schedules

Exact schedules depend on the vet, the region and the pet's lifestyle. Use these as reminders to book an appointment.

## Puppy vaccinations
species: dog | age: puppy | topic: vaccination

Puppies usually get core vaccines at about 6-8 weeks, 10-12 weeks and 14-16 weeks, and a rabies vaccine at about 12-16 weeks. Schedule a vet visit every 3-4 weeks until the series is done.

Source: vaccinations.md, Puppy vaccinations

## Adult dog vaccinations
species: dog | age: adult | topic: vaccination

Adult dogs usually need a yearly checkup with boosters every 1-3 years, depending on the vaccine. Schedule a vet visit once a year.

Source: vaccinations.md, Adult dog vaccinations

## Kitten vaccinations
species: cat | age: kitten | topic: vaccination

Kittens usually get core vaccines at about 6-8 weeks, 10-12 weeks and 14-16 weeks, and a rabies vaccine at about 12-16 weeks. Schedule a vet visit every 3-4 weeks until the series is done.

Source: vaccinations.md, Kitten vaccinations

## Adult cat vaccinations
species: cat | age: adult | topic: vaccination

Adult cats usually need a yearly checkup with boosters every 1-3 years, depending on the vaccine. Schedule a vet visit once a year.

Source: vaccinations.md, Adult cat vaccinations

## Senior checkups
species: all | age: senior | topic: vaccination

Senior pets should see a vet every 6 months for a checkup, including weight and dental checks. Schedule a vet visit twice a year.

Source: vaccinations.md, Senior checkups

## Other species checkups
species: other | age: all | topic: vaccination

Many small pets do not need routine vaccines, but they still benefit from a yearly checkup with a vet who knows the species. Schedule one vet visit a year.

Source: vaccinations.md, Other species checkups
