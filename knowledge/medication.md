# Medication timing rules

## Give medication on a fixed schedule
species: all | age: all | topic: medication

Give medication at the same times each day. For a twice-daily dose, space the doses about 12 hours apart, and for a once-daily dose use the same hour every day. A medication task takes about 5 minutes. Never double a dose to make up for a missed one. Follow the vet's label.

Source: medication.md, Give medication on a fixed schedule

## Medication with food
species: all | age: all | topic: medication

Many medications are given with a meal to avoid stomach upset. Schedule these right after the feeding task (within 15 minutes). Medications that must be given on an empty stomach go at least 1 hour before a meal.

Source: medication.md, Medication with food

## Senior pet medication
species: all | age: senior | topic: medication

Senior pets are more likely to be on long-term medication. Make medication a high-priority daily task, and keep it at a fixed time that is not moved when the schedule is tight.

Source: medication.md, Senior pet medication

## Parasite prevention
species: all | age: all | topic: medication

Flea, tick and worm prevention is usually monthly. Schedule it as a monthly task (about 5 minutes) on the same date each month. Puppies and kittens are usually dewormed more often, every 2-4 weeks until about 12 weeks old.
