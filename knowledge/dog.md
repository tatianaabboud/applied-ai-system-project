# Dog care guidelines

## Puppy feeding
species: dog | age: puppy | topic: feeding

Puppies under 6 months need 3-4 small meals a day, spaced evenly. From 6 to 12 months, 2-3 meals a day is enough. Each feeding takes about 10 minutes, and fresh water should always be available.

Source: dog.md, Puppy feeding

## Adult dog feeding
species: dog | age: adult | topic: feeding

Adult dogs do well on 2 meals a day, about 8-12 hours apart (for example 08:00 and 18:00). Each feeding takes about 10 minutes.

Source: dog.md, Adult dog feeding

## Senior dog feeding
species: dog | age: senior | topic: feeding

Senior dogs usually eat 2 meals a day, and smaller, more frequent meals can help if appetite drops or digestion is sensitive. Check weight and appetite weekly.

Source: dog.md, Senior dog feeding

## Puppy exercise
species: dog | age: puppy | topic: exercise

Puppies need short, frequent outings: about 5 minutes of structured walking per month of age, up to twice a day, plus several short play sessions of 5-10 minutes. Avoid long runs while their joints are growing. Puppies also need house-training trips outside after meals, naps and play.

Source: dog.md, Puppy exercise

## Adult dog exercise
species: dog | age: adult | topic: exercise

Adult dogs need 30-60 minutes of exercise a day, usually split into 2 walks (morning and evening). High-energy breeds such as border collies, huskies and retrievers need 60-120 minutes a day. Small or low-energy breeds can do well with about 30 minutes.

Source: dog.md, Adult dog exercise

## Senior dog exercise
species: dog | age: senior | topic: exercise

Senior dogs benefit from shorter, more frequent walks: 3 walks of 10-20 minutes a day instead of one long walk. Keep the pace gentle, avoid hard jumping, and shorten the walk if the dog lags or limps.

Source: dog.md, Senior dog exercise

## Dog grooming
species: dog | age: all | topic: grooming

Brush short-coated dogs once a week (5-10 minutes) and long-coated dogs daily (10-15 minutes). Brush teeth 3 or more times a week (about 5 minutes). Trim nails every 3-4 weeks (about 10 minutes). Bathe every 4-8 weeks, or when dirty.

Source: dog.md, Dog grooming

## Dog enrichment
species: dog | age: all | topic: enrichment

Dogs need 10-20 minutes of mental activity a day, such as training, puzzle feeders or sniff games. Young dogs and working breeds (for example herding breeds) need more of it.

Source: dog.md, Dog enrichment
